precription
  • Synthetic data: 6 weeks of hourly ED arrivals, with a daily and weekly pattern, train the forecast.
  • Forecast: typical arrivals for each weekday and hour, scaled by a live level that adapts when real arrivals run higher or lower.
  • Patients: arrive with an acuity (1 most urgent). They wait in a queue, get an ED bay, may need CT, and are discharged or admitted. Admitted patients board in the ED until a staffed ward bed exists.
  • Rules: each recommendation is generated from a bottleneck and checked against hard safety limits (nurse ratios, no staff moves out of ICU, Paediatrics or Maternity, only cleared discharges, only stable transfers).
  • Human in the loop: nothing changes until a clinician taps Approve.
Synthetic data for a hackathon demo. Decision support only, not for clinical use.
